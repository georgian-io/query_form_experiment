## Summary

SOCi's 2026 index reports that **LLM queries average ~23 words, roughly 6× traditional search
queries** (~4 words), framed as "search has escaped the search box." Consumer discovery is shifting
from keyword search to conversational, full-sentence natural-language queries.

## Relevance to this study

Convenient **magnitude anchor** for the "~20-word norm for a query put to an assistant" line in the
reframed brief — the ~23-word figure is where the "verbose is ~2× the typical LLM query" comparison
comes from (our verbose runs 45–54 words).

**Handle with care.** Vendor marketing, not a controlled study; the word-count methodology isn't
published and "query" may mean a prompt to a chat assistant rather than a retrieval query. Use only
as a directional/illustrative figure, and pair it with the peer-reviewed geolocation study
([2024-01-18](../2024-01-18-llm-vs-search-geolocation/summary.md)) whenever the number matters. Do
not cite as a load-bearing fact.
